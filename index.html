How to play

MathFun helps you practise your times tables. Here's how:

  1. Set up your player: name, boy or girl, age and a fun character.
  2. Pick Easy, Medium, Hard, or tap Pick a table for one times table.
  3. Tap Play. You'll get 10 questions.
  4. Tap the answer you think is right (or press keys 1-4).
  5. Earn stars, build streaks, and collect badges!

Turn on Timer for a timed challenge, or leave it off for no rush. Sound plays happy effects and Music plays a gentle tune. Open the menu at the top right for your Profile, Rewards, Progress and to switch player.

All your progress stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
