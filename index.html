How to play

MathFun helps you practise your maths. Here's how:

  1. Set up your player: name, boy or girl, age, a theme and a fun character.
  2. Choose what to practise: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication or Division.
  3. Pick how hard: Easy, Medium, Hard or Super Hard (times tables and division also have Pick a number).
  4. Tap Play. You'll get 10 questions.
  5. Tap the answer you think is right (or press keys 1-4).
  6. Earn stars, build streaks, and collect badges!

Try 🌟 Fun Facts for cool things about numbers, or the 🏆 Daily Challenge for a surprise mix of questions. With the Timer off, a 💡 Need a Hint? button pops up if you're stuck for a few seconds.

Pick a 🎨 Theme when you create your profile (or change it any time from the player menu). Themes like Dino Valley, Space Blast or Candy Land change the colours, background, characters, hints and music.

Use the ← arrow to go back a screen, or 🏠 Home (top right) to jump to the operation picker. Open the menu at the top right for your Profile, the 🎨 Theme, the Timer / Sound / Music toggles, your Rewards and Progress, and to switch player.

Number sizes for + and -: Easy = single digit (to 10), Medium = two digits (to 100), Hard = three digits (to 1000), Super Hard = four digits (to 10000).

All your progress stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
