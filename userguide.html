MathFun

User Guide · a playful maths game for kids

What is MathFun? A fun way to practise your maths — addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. Answer questions by tapping one of four choices, and collect stars, streaks and badges as you improve. There's also a Fun Facts tab and a Daily Challenge. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and can be installed like an app to play offline.

Getting started — create a player

The first time you open MathFun, you'll make a player. It asks one thing at a time:

  1. Name — type your first name.
  2. Boy or girl — tap one.
  3. Age — use the − / + buttons or type your age (it starts at 5).
  4. Theme — pick a look and sound (Dino Valley, Space Blast, Candy Land…). The screen changes as you tap.
  5. Character — pick a fun avatar from your chosen theme.

Tap Next to move on, or Back to change something. Each player has their own progress, so brothers, sisters and friends can each have their own.

Pick an option

After picking a player, you'll see six tiles: + Addition − Subtraction × Multiplication ÷ Division 🌟 Fun Facts 🏆 Daily Challenge. Tap an operation to practise it, Fun Facts to read cool facts about numbers, or Daily Challenge for a surprise mix of questions.

Use the ← back arrow to choose a different operation, or the 🏠 Home button in the top-right to jump back to these tiles any time.

Playing a round

Pick how hard you want to play:

  • Addition & Subtraction: Easy single-digit (to 10), Medium two-digit (to 100), Hard three-digit (to 1000), Super Hard four-digit (to 10000). Subtraction never gives a negative answer.
  • Multiplication: Easy tables 1–5, Medium 1–10, Hard 1–20, or Pick a table to focus on one table (1 to 20).
  • Division: Easy up to 5, Medium up to 10, Hard up to 20, or Pick a number to always divide by one number (1 to 20). Answers are always whole numbers.
  1. Tap Play. You'll get 10 questions.
  2. For each question, tap the answer you think is right. On a computer you can also press 1 2 3 4.
  3. You'll see friendly feedback either way, then the next question.

Stuck? With the Timer off, a 💡 Need a Hint? button pops up after a few seconds. Tap it for a helpful tip for that question — it points you the right way without giving the answer.

Themes

MathFun has 10 themes to choose from — Math World (the default), Plumber World, Dino Valley, Speedy Hedgehog, Magic Kingdom, Space Blast, Ocean Deep, Jungle Safari, Candy Land and Robot Lab.

Your theme changes the colours, the background, the little pictures on the tiles and hints, your character choices, and the music. Pick one when you make your player, or change it any time from 🎨 Theme in the player menu (top-right). Changing theme also gives you a new matching character.

The Light / Dark switch works on top of any theme, so each theme has a bright and a dark look.

Timer, Sound & Music

These three switches live in the player menu (tap your avatar, top-right):

  • ⏱️ Timer adds a countdown to each question for a challenge. Leave it off for no rush.
  • 🔊 Sound plays happy sounds for right answers and rewards.
  • 🎵 Music plays an upbeat tune in the background — a different tune for each theme. Music is on to start with.

The game works perfectly with sound and music off — every message is shown on screen too.

Stars, streaks & badges

  • Stars at the end of a round: 3 stars for 10/10, 2 for 8–9, 1 for 6–7.
  • Streaks for answering lots in a row, and for playing on more than one day.
  • Badges for special achievements, like a perfect round or mastering a whole table.

See them any time from My Rewards in the top-right menu.

My Progress

Open My Progress from the top-right menu. Progress is tracked separately for each operation.

If you open it before choosing an operation, you'll see an overview of every operation — rounds played, accuracy and your best score — and you can tap one to see its details. If you open it while you're in an operation, it shows that operation's details straight away.

Every operation shows a summary: rounds played, your accuracy, and your best score at each level. For Multiplication you'll also see a colourful grid of every fact from 1× to 20×. Each square is coloured and labelled:

  • Solid — you know it well.
  • Okay — getting there.
  • Needs work — practise this one more.
  • Not tried — you haven't seen it yet.

MathFun gently gives you more of the multiplication facts you find tricky.

The player menu (top right)

Tap your avatar in the top-right corner for:

  • Profile — see your details, tap Edit to change your name, boy/girl, age or character (or delete the player).
  • Timer, Sound & Music switches.
  • My Rewards and My Progress.
  • Help — a quick reminder of how to play.
  • Switch player — go to the "Who's playing?" screen to change player or add a new one.

Themes & installing

  • The theme button in the header switches between Light and Dark; it starts by following your device's setting, and the button shows which theme a tap will switch to.
  • You can install MathFun to your home screen from your browser's menu ("Add to Home Screen" / "Install app") and then play offline.

Your progress stays on this device only — nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no accounts.
